# The scenes

The 43 scenes in order, with what each one is and does. Times are the timeline's (`app/src/timeline.ts`, computed from the data); `L n` is lyric line n of `data/lyrics.json` (0–29); a sung character named in **bold** is a cue inside the line. "Module" names the scene file when it is not the scene's own (a variant of an earlier scene, in the act's colours). "Text" lists everything on screen besides the slate and the lyric.

Every scene opens on its slate (4 frames) and, unless noted, its lyric sits in the act's rail (`DESIGN.md`).
